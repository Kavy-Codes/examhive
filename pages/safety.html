Before you click

Read once. It takes a minute.

ExamHive is a directory. We list links to sites other people run; we do not host files, streams or downloads. A few habits make this much safer.

1. Keep an ad-blocker on

Most free streams are ad-supported. Pop-ups and "Download here" buttons are often fake. uBlock Origin handles the worst of it. Never install a codec or browser extension a stream asks for.

2. Use a VPN where marked

Some mirrors geo-block or throttle. A link tagged VPN needed was reachable from our checker but may fail from your network. Use a reputable VPN and follow local law.

3. Read the health badge

Working answered the last automated check. Slow responded late or blocked the checker. Down did not respond. Every badge shows when it was checked. Nothing is invented.

4. Watch for fake download buttons

On game and ebook sites, the largest button is frequently an advert. Look for the real download link, read the filename before saving, and scan archives. Prefer sources with install notes.

5. Something broken?

Open the card's Details and tap Report broken. No login. One tap opens a pre-filled email so we can verify and retest. Takedown requests go to krush91@proton.me.

Legal note

Some entertainment links point to copyrighted material and may be restricted in your country. Education, tools and open content are the safest part of this directory. Entertainment entries are user-submitted and listed with that caveat.

ExamHive lists third-party links and hosts nothing. Some third-party links may lead to copyrighted material. Check your local laws and prefer open or legal sources where you can. Education, tools and open content are the point of this site.
