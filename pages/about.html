About ExamHive

Last updated 2026-11-27

What this is

ExamHive is a small, hand-curated directory of free resources. Seven shelves cover exam prep, engineering, books, games, entertainment and site information. There is no account, no login wall and no cross-site tracking. Saved links and your streak stay in this browser.

Who runs it

This site is maintained by an independent student. If you need to reach the website owner about a takedown, a correction, a broken link or a suggestion, email krush91@proton.me. That inbox is monitored.

How link health works

Every link is checked on a schedule by a small script. Working means it answered the last check, Slow means it answered late or blocked the checker, and Down means it did not respond. Each card shows when it was last checked. A link that fails three consecutive checks is hidden from the shelves until it comes back.

Safety and policy

We list third-party links and host no files. Read Before you click for ad-blocker and VPN advice, and the disclaimer for the full policy and takedown process. Want to add something? Submit a link. Submissions are reviewed by the owner before they go live.
