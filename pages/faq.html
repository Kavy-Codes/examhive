FAQ

Updated by the link checker

What is ExamHive?

A small, hand-curated directory of free resources. Six shelves, one search box, and a health badge on every link. We do not host files and there is no account.

How are link statuses decided?

A script checks every link on a schedule and records the result. Working means it answered the last check, Slow means it answered late or blocked the checker, Down means it did not respond. Each card shows when it was checked. Links that fail three consecutive checks are hidden.

Why is a link marked Down but still clickable?

Automated checks can be wrong — sites block bots, go down briefly or geo-restrict. The badge is a signal, not a guarantee. Use the Details button to report a broken link.

Do you store my saved links?

No. Saved links, upvotes and your streak live in your browser's localStorage. No login, no account, no cross-site tracking.

What do the tags mean?

Safe means no obvious pop-up traps in our check, Ads means the site is ad-supported, VPN needed means it may fail from your network, and No signup means you can browse without creating an account there.

How do I report a broken link or request a takedown?

Email krush91@proton.me. Include the link title and URL. Takedown requests are reviewed and actioned. See the disclaimer for the full policy.

Can I suggest a resource?

Yes — submit a link. Submissions are checked manually and then run through the same health script as everything else.

ExamHive lists third-party links and hosts nothing. Some third-party links may lead to copyrighted material. Check your local laws and prefer open or legal sources where you can. Education, tools and open content are the point of this site.
