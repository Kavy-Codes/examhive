How to use it

  • Biology — stick to NCERT lines first, then use notes for revision.
  • Chemistry — keep a separate formula sheet and test it weekly.
  • Physics — solve numericals daily rather than collecting more PDFs.
  • Tests — timed practice beats another round of highlight-and-reread.
